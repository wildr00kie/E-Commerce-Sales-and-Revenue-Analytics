# E-Commerce Sales & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This enterprise-grade E-Commerce Sales Analytics project evaluates revenue trends, product performance, order dynamics, and regional market distribution across ₹3,328.5 Cr in total sales. Utilizing data-driven diagnostics across category volume splits, Pareto 80/20 distributions, order size concentration, and geographical spend patterns, this dashboard provides commercial leaders with actionable insights to optimize inventory allocation, pricing strategies, and regional expansion.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Slide Highlights

### 1. Revenue Trends & Category Performance
* **Monthly Revenue Trend with Moving Average**: Identifies long-term trajectory and seasonal momentum by smoothing short-term sales fluctuations with moving averages.
* **Category Revenue Breakdown**: Highlights severe category skew, driven heavily by Electronics (75%+ revenue share) and Furniture.
* **Revenue Growth Rate**: Tracks period-over-period expansion metrics to evaluate underlying commercial trajectory across business quarters.
* **Top 10 & Bottom 10 Products**: Ranks top revenue drivers alongside low-performing SKUs to inform assortment rationalization and inventory clearance strategies.

### 2. Product Economics & Volume Matrices
* **Quantity Sold vs. Sales Revenue Matrix (Treemap)**: Visualizes revenue concentration against total units moved, distinguishing high-volume low-margin items from premium high-value SKUs.
* **Product Price Distribution (Histogram)**: Maps pricing tier frequency across the product catalog to evaluate price point elasticity and portfolio structuring.
* **Category Volume vs. Revenue Split**: Contrasts unit sales volume against gross sales value to pinpoint high-margin, revenue-generating categories versus high-volume operational categories.

### 3. Regional Market Dynamics & Order Analytics
* **Sales Distribution Map by City**: Maps geographic revenue footprint, demonstrating geographic diversification and localized demand density.
* **Top Cities Revenue Breakdown Stacked Bar**: Ranks primary urban markets showing consistent revenue contribution (~₹126–130 Cr) across top-tier cities.
* **Average Spend per Order by City**: Evaluates purchasing power across regional markets, maintaining strong Average Order Value consistency (~₹29.5K–30.8K) across top locations.
* **Order Size Distribution**: Identifies basket size behavior, showing order volume peaking at 3 items per transaction (~2.2L orders).
* **Pareto Analysis Chart**: Validates the 80/20 commercial rule, proving that Electronics and Furniture combined drive 84.26% of overall company revenue.
* **Daily Sales Volatility Scatter Plot**: Captures strong correlation between daily order count and total revenue while highlighting daily volume swings (~₹2.8 Cr to ₹4.2 Cr).
